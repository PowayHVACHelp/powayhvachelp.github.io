Questions to ask an HVAC contractor in Poway, CA

Hiring someone to fix or replace an air conditioner or furnace is easier when you know what to ask. These are the questions that tell you the most in the shortest call.

Before they come out

  • What is your California C-20 license number? You can look it up on the Contractors State License Board site in a minute.
  • Do you carry liability insurance and workers' compensation?
  • Is there a charge for the visit or the diagnosis, and does it go toward the repair?
  • Do you work on my type of system: central AC, heat pump, gas furnace or ductless?

When you get a price

  • Can I have the estimate in writing, with parts and labor listed?
  • Is this a repair that should last, or a short-term fix on an old system?
  • For a replacement, how did you size the new equipment for my house?
  • Will you pull the permit if the work needs one?

Warranty and follow-up

  • What warranty covers the parts, and what covers your labor?
  • Do I need to register the equipment with the manufacturer to keep the warranty?
  • Who do I call if something goes wrong after the job?
